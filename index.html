Reference-Guided Legged-Wheel Locomotion on Stairs

This page collects representative videos and figures from a simulated stair-climbing study using Proximal Policy Optimization, body and wheel reference trajectories, and curriculum learning in Isaac Lab.

Project Summary

The thesis studies whether a policy can use predefined body and wheel reference trajectories to learn stair traversal with a legged-wheel robot. The policy receives proprioceptive observations and a compact reference command. A critic uses simulator-only privileged information during training.

Main Result

The best tested reward balance weighted three-reference tracking above the base-task posture objective. With the default ELU-based PPO setup, the learned policy completed all evaluated stair levels in simulation.
